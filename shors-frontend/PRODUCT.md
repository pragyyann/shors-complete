# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Urban youth aged 18–32 in India and the UK diaspora. They are fashion-forward, bold, and anti-generic tastemakers looking for high-quality, culturally expressive statement apparel.

## Product Purpose
To showcase premium, culturally expressive canvas tote bags rooted in Indian heritage. The website acts as a high-end catalog, brand showcase, and pre-order portal that drives excitement and gathers registrations for exclusive drops.

## Brand Personality
"HERITAGE IN MOTION". Unapologetic, loud, maximalist, and confident, blending rich Indian heritage with modern high-fashion editorial aesthetics.

## Anti-references
- Flat, minimal, clinical SaaS platforms
- Standard template Shopify grids
- Bright pastel or neon-lit commercial web styles
- Tiny, uniform geometric sans-serif layouts

## Design Principles
- **Heritage in Motion**: Blend deep cultural heritage motifs with modern dynamic layouts.
- **Unapologetic Contrast**: Employ deep editorial dark backgrounds (wine, charcoal) with statement gold and vibrant accents.
- **Expressive Typography**: Use bold, oversized serif and display typography to command attention and convey premium editorial design.
- **Seamless Motion**: Incorporate smooth, fluid animations while respecting user preferences for reduced motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Adherence to WCAG AA color contrast guidelines where possible.
- Strict support for user accessibility preferences, specifically `prefers-reduced-motion` animations.
